Two ways to order

However you'd rather do it

Some customers already have the fabric. Others want us to handle everything. ZEBAYSH does both — the finished piece is stitched to your measurements and style either way.

Path A

Send your own fabric

  • 01Send us the fabric you already own, along with a reference design or your own idea.
  • 02We confirm your measurements and go over cut, style and finishing details with you.
  • 03Our tailors cut and stitch the piece to your exact fit.
  • 04Your finished garment is packed and delivered to your door.

Path B

Let us source it for you

  • 01Tell us the fabric type, color and style you have in mind — or leave it to us.
  • 02We purchase the fabric on your behalf, exactly as discussed.
  • 03It's tailored to your measurements, just like a bring-your-own order.
  • 04Delivered finished and ready to wear — no extra steps for you.
